## 1. Dual-Licensing Model Overview

The ACTOVRA project is publicly distributed as open-source software under the terms of the **GNU Affero General Public License Version 3 or later** ([AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE)).

The GNU AGPLv3 is a strong network copyleft license designed to ensure that improvements to network server software remain open to the public community. Under AGPLv3, any organization or individual that modifies the software and makes it available to users over a computer network (such as a Software-as-a-Service or cloud platform) is obligated to make the complete corresponding source code available to all network users under the same license terms.

## 3. Commercial Licensing Terms & Arrangements

- **Independently Negotiated:** Commercial licenses are negotiated separately between the copyright holder and the licensee. Commercial terms, pricing, and agreements are entirely separate from, and do not modify or amend, the public GNU AGPLv3 license.
- **Flexible Structures:** Commercial arrangements may be structured according to business needs, including annual enterprise licenses, platform subscriptions, fixed-fee deployment licenses, or negotiated royalty agreements.
- **Scope Limitation:** A commercial license from the copyright holder covers **only ACTOVRA-owned source code**. Third-party dependencies, libraries, and runtime components utilized by the project remain subject to their respective upstream licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache-2.0, LGPL-3.0). The copyright holder does not relicense third-party software.
